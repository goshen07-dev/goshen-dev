Your data is used exclusively for the following purposes:

· To respond to your contact form inquiries and project requests.

05 / Security

Data Storage & Security

All data is stored on Render's infrastructure with PostgreSQL databases protected by environment variable encryption. We apply the following security measures:

· All data transmission is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

· Database credentials and API keys are stored as encrypted environment variables, never in source code.

· Access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel only.

· We conduct regular security reviews of our codebase and dependencies.

Data is retained only as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected. Contact data from inquiries is retained for a maximum of 24 months unless a contractual relationship requires longer retention.

06 / Your Rights

Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We are committed to honoring all requests within 30 days.

👁️

Right to Access

Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.

✏️

Right to Rectification

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.

🗑️

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").

⏸️

Right to Restriction

Request that we limit how we process your data.

📦

Right to Portability

Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

🚫

Right to Object

Object to processing based on legitimate interest at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@goshen-dev.com with the subject line "GDPR Request". We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

07 / Cookies

Cookies

Our main portfolio website (goshen-dev.onrender.com) uses only essential session cookies required for basic functionality. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies on this site.

Our web applications may use session cookies to maintain your authenticated state. These cookies are strictly necessary and do not track you across other websites.

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences at any time without affecting the basic functionality of this portfolio site.
